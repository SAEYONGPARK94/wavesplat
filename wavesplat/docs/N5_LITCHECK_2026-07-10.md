# N5 — Litcheck for the derived architecture (three primitives)

2026-07-10. Standing rule: **no novelty claim before litcheck.** The sibling
agent's architecture has three primitives:

- **P1** — activation = the **truncated-Gaussian EP moment-match** (a
  physics-derived nonlinearity that reduces exactly to the linear solver at zero
  site precision).
- **P2** — every learned correction is **multiplied by the computed posterior
  contraction** `c_i = γ_i·φᵢᵀK⁻¹φᵢ` (a parameter-free "information gate" that
  makes out-of-support memorization architecturally impossible).
- **P3** — **wave-group equivariance**: complex amplitudes on the normalized
  dispersion shell, phase-rotation-equivariant complex-linear layers, `k/kp`
  scale built in.

Searches run (multiple queries each): deep-unfolding/unrolled EP; learned EP
messages (Heess 2013 + newer); KalmanNet / physics-fixed-transition learned
filters; gating by uncertainty/Fisher/posterior-variance; certified/provable-
constraint nets; complex phase-equivariant nets; steerable equivariant nets on
manifolds/shells; inequality-constrained GP + EP + truncated-Gaussian moment
match; amortized Bayesian experimental design (Foster/DAD); learned active-set
selection for GP/EP; EP-as-differentiable-layer / moment-matching activation;
amortized posterior-contraction / information-gain prediction.

## Concluding paragraph (plain)

There is **no principle-level novelty in any single primitive**, and the litcheck
should be reported that way. P1 (truncated-Gaussian EP moment-match as a
differentiable activation that collapses to the Gaussian/linear solver at zero
site precision) is the standard EP site update plus the well-worn
"moment-matching-as-a-layer" and "deep-unfolding EP" lines. P3 (phase-rotation-
equivariant complex-linear layers, scale built in, on a curved shell) is textbook
equivariant-net construction — bias-free complex-linear = phase-equivariant is
established, and steerable/gauge nets on spheres and homogeneous spaces are a
mature subfield; only the *choice of manifold* (the dispersion shell) is new, and
that is a domain binding. P2 is the one primitive worth pausing on: gating every
learned correction by the **computed, parameter-free posterior contraction**
`γφᵀK⁻¹φ` so that corrections vanish out of support and the network provably
reverts to the classical estimator is a genuinely tidy guarantee — but it is a
**composition** of ingredients that all exist (KalmanNet-style learned-gain-inside-
a-fixed-recursion; leverage-score / information-filter weighting; hard-constrained-
by-construction nets like HardNet/CAffNet), so it is an *engineering guarantee*,
not a new learning principle. **The defensible claim is compositional + empirical
only:** a physics-specified estimator that (i) unifies these three known
mechanisms into one architecture derived end-to-end from the wave-sensor
likelihood, (ii) carries a by-construction no-extrapolation guarantee through the
`γφᵀK⁻¹φ` gate, and (iii) is validated on the LiDAR wave-field task including HOS
nonlinear and real-data transfer. Write "we compose known primitives into a
physics-derived estimator with a by-construction information gate, and show it
empirically on X" — **do not write "novel activation / novel gate / novel
equivariance."** Given this project's history of severe penalties for inflated
claims, "no principle novelty; compositional + empirical contribution" is the
correct and safe framing. If any one sentence is pushed as the sharpest, it is P2
phrased as a *guarantee* ("out-of-support memorization is architecturally
impossible via a zero-parameter contraction gate"), not as a *new mechanism* —
and even that must cite HardNet/KalmanNet/PASS-GP as the composed antecedents.
