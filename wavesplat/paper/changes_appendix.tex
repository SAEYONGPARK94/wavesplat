\section{Changes from earlier versions}
\label{app:changes}

Earlier versions of this manuscript made four claims that later measurement
withdrew, and withdrew one evaluation corpus outright. We list them here rather than in the running text, so that the body states
only what is currently supported; each is referenced from the section where the
claim used to appear.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering
\footnotesize
\caption{Withdrawals and corrections, with what replaced them.}
\label{tab:changes}

\toprule
& Withdrawn & Why, and what stands now \\
\midrule
\textbf{R1} & Geometry-aware confidence weighting as a \emph{contribution}
(2026-08-14) &
A fresh out-of-sample corpus found disabling the weighting better in both strata.
The mechanism is still deployed and still described, because a later ablation
found that \emph{removing} the ground-truth-derived weighting also hurts; what
does not stand is calling it a contribution of this work. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{R2} & ``The ground truth is Gerstner (non-linear), so the
$O(\epsilon^2)$ mismatch affects all methods equally'' (2026-08-14) &
False on both halves. The generator discards the horizontal displacement, so the
reference field is a displacement-free linear superposition and the measured
mismatch against the model equation is exactly zero. The non-linear case is now
measured separately with a higher-order reference
(Section~\ref{sec:res_hos}). \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{R3} & All absolute per-frame latencies (2026-08-14) &
They came from separate processes without thread pinning, so they measured the
harness as much as the estimator. \emph{Superseded}: the deployed path has since
been timed in one process on a single thread and is reported in
Section~\ref{sec:complexity}. No baseline arm was timed, so no comparative timing
claim replaces the withdrawn ones. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{R4} & ``The dispersion constraint contributes accuracy''
(2026-08-14) &
The ablation that supported it removed the frequency initialisation together with
the constraint, so it did not isolate either. What stands is the parameter-count
reduction, which is structural. A controlled intervention has not been run. \\

\textbf{R5} & The \emph{corpus} itself: every cross-arm number computed on the
$100$-episode evaluation dataset (2026-08-14) &
This is not a withdrawal of a claim but of a dataset. That corpus was found to score
the compared arms on \emph{different frames}, so no cross-arm comparison on it means
what it appears to mean. Section~\ref{sec:res_summary} states which quantities had a
pre-registered replacement and which are simply absent as a result; the withdrawn
sections, tables and figures are kept verbatim in the supplementary archive named in
the Data availability statement rather than deleted, which is what
``archived'' means at every use of that word in this paper. \\

\textbf{R6} & Two descriptions of the tracking limitation: a beamforming detection
threshold at $|k|\gtrsim 0.4$~rad/m, and a $2$--$3\times$ inflation of tracking error
over post-initialisation error (2026-08) &
A mesh-independent test of the initialiser alone --- synthesising samples directly
from analytic components with the real sensor geometry, bypassing the raycast mesh
--- found no such threshold: isolated components are recovered across
$|k| = 0.15$--$1.00$~rad/m down to $0.01$~m amplitude. The value $0.4$~rad/m is
$\lambda \approx 15.7$~m, where amplitude transmission through the \emph{superseded}
rendering mesh collapses. Both figures were properties of the reference field and of
the uncorrected tracker, and neither reproduces. The structural limitation stands and
is stated in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{R7} & Adam momentum as the cause of residual amplitude
under-estimation (2026-08-24) &
The attribution named an explicit phase advance $\phi_i \mathrel{+}= \omega_i \Delta t$
as the source of the oscillating gradients. That path is switched off in the shipped
configuration, so the named cause no longer exists. The deficit itself is still
observed and is now reported as \emph{unattributed}. \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{R8} & The magnitudes of the $\Delta k_{\min}$ sensitivity sweep (2026-08) &
The sweep used $5$ seeds per cell with the decision rule fixed in advance and a
control episode containing no close pair. Relaxing the radius to $0.05$~rad/m
improved the crowded $15$-component case on every seed and degraded the
$5$-component case on every seed --- a smaller radius lets the greedy search stack
duplicate peaks on one true component --- while the control episode was unaffected.
The \emph{direction} stands and is stated in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}; the
levels are not restated because the sweep is from the withdrawn corpus (R5) and has
not been re-measured. Separately, for $N_{\text{true}}\geq 30$ the wavenumber-space
crowding exceeds what the beamforming grid resolves at any separation radius, so an
adaptive radius alone would not close that regime. \\
