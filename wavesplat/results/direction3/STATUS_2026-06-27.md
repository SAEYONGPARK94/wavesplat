# WaveSplat PhD research — status summary (2026-06-27)

One-line thesis: **phase-resolved wave estimation from sparse ship-LiDAR is
information-limited; we prove the limit (CRB) and show the concrete way past it is
not a better single-window estimator but information-gathering by design (active
sensing + sequential time fusion), with a learned prior providing the mandatory
bias and calibrated uncertainty.**

## Headline numbers (30-episode benchmark, field nRMSE)
| method | nRMSE | note |
|---|---|---|
| ST-DF-GP (prior SOTA, single 1 s window) | 0.205 | superb on simple seas, fails on complex |
| grid-free DPS (single window) | 0.220 | complexity-robust + calibrated UQ |
| **CRB-gated hybrid** | **0.174** | **−15% vs SOTA**, ≈ oracle 0.171 |
| **sequential time fusion (10–15 s, complex seas)** | **0.02–0.03** | breaks the wall |

## Direction I — CRB / information limits (theory backbone) ✅
- Dispersion-constrained space-time Fisher information for sparse ship-LiDAR
  (analytic Jacobian verified to 1e-15).
- **Effective aperture** D_eff = L_footprint + c_g·T_obs (synthetic aperture);
  σ_|k| ∝ 1/D_eff verified (slope −1.09). Synthetic aperture sharpens |k|/ω, not
  direction (cross-range-limited).
- **Information wall** T_wall (CRB < zero-predictor): 5c 0.1 s / 15c 4.3 s / 30c 9.7 s.
- **Predictable zone = identifiability, not geometry** — for the parametric model the
  predictability boundary is set by which components are observable, not the
  geometric c_g-cone (distinguishes from classical DSWP).
- Estimators near-optimal where identifiable; CRB diverges where under-determined ⇒
  bias/learned-prior is mandatory below the wall.

## Direction II — learned prior, grid (earlier) ✅/❌
- Diffusion spectral prior: concept **GO** (MLE 1.000 → 0.294) but grid **NO-GO**
  (64×64 quantization costs +0.22).

## Direction III — grid-free learned prior ✅
- **Atomic set-diffusion prior + DPS** with the dispersion-constrained GP-marginal
  likelihood (atoms keep the likelihood exact/continuous — cures the grid NO-GO).
- Beats the grid: BMA 0.248 < grid-diffusion 0.294 < even grid-oracle 0.282.
- **Scaled "본격" training (1.56M, 40k, 600 ep, EMA, min-SNR):** val ε-loss floor
  irreducible (~0.11) ⇒ field result barely moves (0.248→0.240). **Decisive proof the
  bottleneck is information, not training/capacity.**
- **Integrated 30-ep:** DPS complexity-robust (flat 0.19–0.23), wins all 4 complex
  multidirectional sea-states; ST-DF-GP wins simple seas (overall 0.220 vs 0.205).
  **UQ calibrated** (one global temperature s=1.38 → ±1σ 0.68, ±2σ 0.95).
- **Honest critique (persona debate):** the DPS BMA over-smooths (variance ratio
  0.17–0.33); the complex-sea "win" is graceful (skill ≥ 0) vs catastrophic
  (ST-DF-GP skill < 0) failure, not true reconstruction.

## Improvement program (advisor wanted improvement, not "honest failure")
1. **CRB-gated hybrid (PROVEN WIN):** route precise ST-DF-GP (identifiable) vs robust
   DPS (under-determined) by the FIM condition number (separates by 15 orders).
   **0.174, −15% vs SOTA, ≈ oracle.** Turns the limit into a model-selection rule.
   - *Deployable gate (step 1):* 5 data-only single-window signals all FAIL ⇒
     **identifiability is not self-diagnosable from one window** (deepest form of the
     wall). Hybrid still deployable via onboard sea-state metadata (directional spread)
     or temporal stability.
2. **CRB-optimal active sensing (step 2):** D-optimal return selection cuts achieved
   nRMSE on the complex sea **0.204→0.138 (−32%)**; deployable cross-range-spread rule
   keeps −20%. Scan/return design = a real, deployable lever.
3. **Sequential time fusion (step 3 — the decisive lever):** accumulating ~10–15 s of
   track reconstructs the *hardest* seas to **0.02–0.03**, far below single-window
   methods and the old single-window "exact" 0.06 floor. ST-DF-GP fuses only 1 s
   (below T_wall) — exactly why it fails on complex seas.

## Negative results (done right, all unified by the CRB)
- NN as a direct **estimator** (T-NSE, TNP/TE-TNP, ConvCNP, WaveNP, DDKL, WavePINO,
  KalmanNet): all fail / collapse to mean — CRB explains why (no stable target below
  the wall).
- Scaling the prior 10×: inert (information floor).
- Post-hoc sharpening (max-lik sample, off-grid refine): restores amplitude but wrecks
  structure — you can't sharpen past the wall.

## Contribution framing (from the persona debate)
- **Strongest / most novel:** the CRB information-wall theory + predictable-zone =
  identifiability. *Lead the paper with this.*
- **Method:** grid-free atomic set-diffusion + physics-DPS — novel assembly,
  complexity-robust + calibrated UQ; value is graceful degradation + honest UQ.
- **Improvement / SOTA-beating:** CRB-gated hybrid (−15%) + active sensing + sequential
  fusion — *information-gathering by design* is the way past the wall.
- Honest verdict: as a "beat-SOTA single-window estimator" paper it is marginal; as a
  **"limits of the problem + how to beat them by design + calibrated UQ"** paper it is
  novel and defensible.

## Verified vs open
- **Verified:** CRB theory; grid-free DPS; hybrid potential (oracle gate); active-
  sensing lever (CRB→achieved); sequential-fusion lever.
- **Open / implementation:** deployable gate (sea-state metadata or temporal); the
  *actual* sequential estimator without true components; real-data validation;
  multi-frame evaluation.
