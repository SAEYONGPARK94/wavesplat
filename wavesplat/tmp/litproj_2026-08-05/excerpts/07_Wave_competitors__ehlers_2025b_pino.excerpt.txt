For intermediate to deep water, the nonlinear potential flow theory is commonly used to model surface gravity waves and can be solved with various numerical methods1–4.

To solve for W, the HOSM expresses the velocity potential and vertical velocity as perturbation series in wave steepness Φ(x,z,t) = M ∑ m=1 Φ(m)(x,z,t) (3) W(x,t) = M ∑ m=1 W (m)(x,t) (4) up to a predefined order of nonlinearity M.

The initial elevations are sampled from a JONSWAP TMA spectrum49,50 with random phases, a peak enhancement factor γ = 3.3 and various combinations of peak wavelength Lp ∈{100, 110,...,200}m and wave steepness ε ∈{0.02,0.03,...,0.13}.

A relaxation period of 20 peak periods precedes data collection to allow the development of nonlinear wave dynamics.
