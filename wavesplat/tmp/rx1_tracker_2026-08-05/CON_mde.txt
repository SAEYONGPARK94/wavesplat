================================================================================================
MDE-3 — what the K1-K5 knobs must beat to be readable at all
================================================================================================
  total tracker-attributable error at oracle = 0.1027
  MDE of the design                          = 0.0269
  => a knob must remove >= 26% of the tracker term to be distinguishable from seed noise at 4/4.
  A NULL on this design therefore only excludes knobs worth more than 0.0269 nRMSE;
  it does NOT license "regularisation was not causal".
