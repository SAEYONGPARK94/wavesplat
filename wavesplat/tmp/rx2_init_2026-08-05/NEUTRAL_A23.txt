A2 — MDE of the Rx-2 design (n=4 paired episodes, median-of-3 seeds/cell, two-sided sign test)
========================================================================================================
Power model: the sign test can only reach its floor p=0.125 at 4/4.  For 80% power we need
  P(correct sign per episode) = 0.80^(1/4) = 0.9457  ->  z = 1.6035
  MDE = 1.6035 * SD(paired median-of-3 delta).  SD is bootstrapped over the 3 seeds,
  RESAMPLING THE SAME SEEDS IN BOTH ARMS so the seed pairing (and its correlation) is kept.
