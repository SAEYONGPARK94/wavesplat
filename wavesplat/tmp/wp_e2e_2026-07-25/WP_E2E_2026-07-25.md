## 1. PRE-REGISTERED VERDICT — **PRIMARY FAIL (null)**

Primary: 40 crowded finemesh episodes, proposal-level 'cond' nowcast nRMSE/Hs (median-of-5 sampling
seeds), **fine-tuned (chosen config A) vs current checkpoint**, paired one-sided Wilcoxon
(H1: ft < current), α=0.05.

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| ft mean (nRMSE/Hs) | **0.1244** |
| current mean | 0.1245 |
| median paired Δ (ft − current) | **−0.0007** (−0.6%) |
| mean paired Δ | −0.0002 |
| wins (ft better) | **21 / 40** |
| rank-biserial | +0.098 |
| one-sided Wilcoxon p | **0.300** |
| **P1 (p<0.05)** | ❌ False |
| **P2 (median Δ<0)** | ✅ True |
| **DECISION** | **FAIL** |

P1 fails → **FAIL**. The effect is negligible (median Δ −0.0007, mean −0.0002, means identical to
4 decimals) and non-significant. **Fine-tuning the proposal against a downstream field loss does NOT
beat the current checkpoint at the proposal level.** This is the **third retrain-failure pattern**
(after γ and bank-MMSE) reproduced on the e2e axis — anticipated in the PREREG as a known risk and
reported faithfully.

**Default-path / baseline integrity (checked BEFORE readout, PREREG §4):** the current ckpt re-run
through *this* harness reproduced the recorded head-to-head cond numbers to **Δ=0.0000 on 3/3**
episodes → the paired baseline is valid; the FAIL is not a protocol artifact.

## 3. The train/eval distribution gap — WHERE the fine-tune gain washes out (MEASURED)

The chosen config **did** improve on the held-out **synthetic** validation set, but that gain did
**not** transfer to the finemesh episodes:

| set | current | ft (A) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| held-out synthetic val (16 scenes, nc∈{15,30}, median field nRMSE) | 0.1749 | **0.1597** | **−0.0152** |
| finemesh crowded 40 (median cond nRMSE/Hs) | 0.1245 | 0.1244 | −0.0001 |

The fine-tune learned a **real** improvement (−0.015, ~9%) on the `make_batch`-style obs
distribution it was trained on (random footprint points, U[0,Tobs] times, linear forward), but that
improvement **evaporates** on the finemesh eval episodes, whose obs come from the actual LiDAR sweep
geometry / tracking-window timing / finer GT mesh via `gather_frames`. The gain is specific to the
synthetic obs-generation protocol, not the episode protocol — the fine-tune did not move the net on
the axis the eval measures. (MEASURED: both numbers are same-protocol paired.)

## 4. Config selection (frozen rule) + training-curve sanity

Selection = lower **median held-out-synthetic-val field nRMSE** (PREREG §3), never touching the eval
episodes:

| config | λ_field | anchor loss 0→end | field loss 0→end | val median | selected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** (light) | 1.0 | 0.207 → 0.220 | 0.306 → 0.122 | **0.1597** | ✅ chosen |
| B (strong) | 4.0 | 0.207 → **0.366** | 0.306 → 0.143 | 0.2417 | ❌ rejected |
| current | — | — | — | 0.1749 | (reference) |

**Config B is a clean negative in its own right:** the strong field weight (λ=4) drove the field
loss down but **wrecked the denoising anchor** (0.207→0.366), and the frozen full-pipeline val
correctly caught it — B's ancestral sampler degraded to 0.242, *worse* than the un-fine-tuned ckpt.
Lesson (ARGUED→MEASURED): the field loss must be a **light auxiliary** to the ε-MSE anchor, not a
dominant term, or it detaches the net from being a valid DDPM denoiser.

## 7. Route through the DDPM-sampler blocker (chosen) + why

**Chosen: x̂₀-prediction FIELD-CONSISTENCY fine-tune** (blocker #1's "supervise the x̂₀-prediction
directly"). The eval PRIMARY runs the *identical frozen ancestral DDPM sampler + polish +
pool_and_select* on the fine-tuned weights, so fine-tuning must improve the ε-predictor's implicit
x̂₀ decode — which every ancestral step computes. Loss = ε-MSE anchor (all samples/all t → preserves
the generative model + the energy channel `pool_and_select` uses) + λ·field-MSE on the low-t x̂₀
decode (denorm → **differentiable torch ridge-LS** solves w/φ on the obs → render on a held-out grid
→ (RMSE/Hs)² vs true field). Because w/φ are an LS function of kx/ky and amplitude is re-solved, the
field gradient concentrates on **kx/ky placement** — the Stage-1 mechanism quantity.

**Why this one (minimal risk):** no change to the eval sampler (apples-to-apples eval), no
distillation / new head (checkpoint is an identical `CondSetDenoiser` state_dict; `learned_init.py`
/ precompute / `run_cond_crowded` load it unchanged), no backprop through 500 stochastic no_grad
steps. Rejected: (a) few-step DDIM-with-grad — heavier and diverges from the 500-step ancestral eval
(train/eval mismatch); (b) distill-to-set-prediction-head — bigger architecture change, breaks
apples-to-apples eval, higher failure risk. Verified working: dispersion parity 2e-16, gradients
flow through the full net→x̂₀→LS→render→loss path.

## 8. MEASURED vs ARGUED

**MEASURED (this test):**
- Proposal-level: ft (chosen config A) does NOT beat the current ckpt on the 40 crowded finemesh
  episodes — median Δ −0.0007, 21/40 wins, p=0.30 → FAIL. Uniform null across all 6 regime splits.
- Baseline reproducibility Δ=0.0000 (3/3): the FAIL is real, not an artifact.
- The fine-tune produced a real +9% (−0.015 median) improvement on held-out **synthetic** val
  (make_batch obs distribution) that does **not** transfer to the finemesh episode obs distribution.
- Fine-tuning does NOT worsen OOD; the two BF2_1comp catastrophes improved (−0.032, −0.037).
- Integrated-arm: ft-learned vs old-learned median Δ +0.0005, 4/10, p=0.35 — gains do not clearly
  propagate through the tracker.
- Config B (λ_field=4) degrades the denoiser (anchor 0.21→0.37, val 0.175→0.242); the frozen
  full-pipeline selection rule correctly rejected it.

**ARGUED (supported, not proven):**
- The field-loss fine-tune's failure to transfer is a **train/eval obs-distribution gap**: the net
  optimizes placement well on the synthetic obs sampling it trains on, but the episode obs
  (structured LiDAR sweeps, finer GT mesh) do not benefit. Strongly supported by the val-vs-finemesh
  contrast on the identical metric, but "obs-distribution gap" is an inference, not a directly
  isolated cause (a matched-obs ablation would confirm).
- The proposal net is likely already near the ceiling of what the beamform-conditioned prior can
  place on these obs; an amortized field-loss nudge cannot extract more without either (a) matching
  the fine-tune obs distribution to the episode protocol or (b) a heavier through-tracker meta-grad.

## 9. Next-step recommendation

**Ship Stage-1's modular result as the product.** Stage-2 bounds "e2e fine-tuning helps at the
proposal level" to a **null** on the finemesh episodes; there is no e2e win to add. If the axis is
revisited, the single highest-value change is to **match the fine-tune obs distribution to the
episode protocol** (train scenes drawn via `gather_frames`-style LiDAR sweep geometry rather than
`make_batch` random footprint sampling) — the val-vs-finemesh gap (§3) localizes the leak there, and
that is a data-generation change, not a bigger model. Through-tracker meta-gradients remain a larger,
separate effort and are not motivated by this null. **No default flip, no paper edit, no checkpoint
promotion** — scope unchanged from Stage-1 §7.
